# Demo Product Guide
**Synthetic sample material for demo use only.**
This document is fictional and does not describe any real institution, customer, or product.

## Overview
This guide describes three fictional financing products used in a lightweight business banking advisory demo.

### FlexLine Demo
**Purpose:** Short-term working capital flexibility.

**Typical use cases:**
- seasonal cash flow pressure
- temporary supplier prepayments
- timing mismatch between expenses and incoming customer payments

**Typical customer profile:**
- established small or medium-sized business
- recurring revenue base
- no unresolved tax arrears
- latest financial statements available

**Indicative range:** EUR 50,000 to EUR 250,000

**Basic guidance:**
- usually intended for businesses with at least 2 years in operation
- positive or near-break-even profitability is preferred
- repeated payment delays weaken preliminary fit
- not intended to replace long-term investment financing

### InvoiceBridge Demo
**Purpose:** Financing against eligible business-to-business receivables.

**Typical use cases:**
- customer invoices with standard payment terms
- growing sales creating working capital pressure
- businesses with reliable B2B billing patterns

**Typical customer profile:**
- meaningful share of invoicing to business customers
- visible invoice cycle and receivables base
- latest financial statements available
- no unresolved tax arrears

**Indicative range:** EUR 40,000 to EUR 300,000

**Basic guidance:**
- best aligned when a large share of revenue comes from B2B invoicing
- customer concentration should be reviewed if one buyer dominates revenue
- not intended for consumer receivables-heavy businesses
- repeated payment delays may require escalation

### AssetGrow Demo
**Purpose:** Equipment and machinery investment support.

**Typical use cases:**
- production equipment purchase
- warehouse automation
- logistics fleet renewal
- business-critical machinery investment

**Typical customer profile:**
- investment linked to business operations
- reasonable balance sheet strength
- latest financial statements available
- positive EBITDA preferred

**Indicative range:** EUR 100,000 to EUR 600,000

**Basic guidance:**
- usually intended for businesses with at least 3 years in operation
- positive EBITDA in latest period is preferred
- weak equity ratio may reduce preliminary fit
- unresolved tax arrears block a confident positive preliminary view

## General exclusions
A confident positive preliminary fit should not be stated when:
- latest financial statements are missing
- unresolved tax arrears are present
- the requested purpose does not match the product purpose
- there is not enough information to compare the customer against the guidance

## Advisor reminder
This guide supports preliminary discussion only. It is not a credit decision and must not be presented as approval guidance.
