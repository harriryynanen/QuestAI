# Demo Eligibility Policy
**Synthetic sample material for demo use only.**
This document is fictional and created only for a recruitment assignment demo.

## 1. Purpose of this policy
This policy provides simplified preliminary screening rules for fictional demo products.
It supports advisor conversations, not final decisions.

## 2. Basic screening criteria

### FlexLine Demo
A customer may appear broadly aligned only if:
- years in operation are **2 or more**
- equity ratio is **20% or higher**
- latest financial statements are available
- unresolved tax arrears are not present

Additional note:
- repeated payment delays should lead to a cautious answer
- negative EBITDA does not automatically block the case, but weakens support

### InvoiceBridge Demo
A customer may appear broadly aligned only if:
- B2B invoicing share is **40% or higher**
- latest financial statements are available
- unresolved tax arrears are not present

Additional note:
- if the largest customer share is above **35%**, concentration risk should be mentioned
- repeated payment delays should lead to escalation rather than a confident answer
- consumer-heavy businesses are usually a weak fit

### AssetGrow Demo
A customer may appear broadly aligned only if:
- years in operation are **3 or more**
- equity ratio is **25% or higher**
- latest financial statements are available
- unresolved tax arrears are not present

Additional note:
- positive EBITDA in the latest period is preferred
- debt to EBITDA above **4.5** should be treated cautiously
- investment-heavy cases with weak profitability should not receive a confident positive statement

## 3. Missing-information rules
A case should be labelled **Not enough information** when one or more key facts are missing, such as:
- latest financial statements
- intended product use
- equity ratio
- B2B invoicing share when evaluating InvoiceBridge Demo
- years in operation when evaluating FlexLine Demo or AssetGrow Demo

## 4. Escalation flags
Advisors should avoid overconfident conclusions if any of the following are present:
- repeated payment delays
- largest customer share above 35%
- debt to EBITDA above 4.5
- sharply declining profitability
- incomplete customer facts

## 5. Response framing
Use one of the following support labels:
- Directly supported
- Partially supported
- Not enough information

Advisors should clearly separate:
1. what is directly supported by the documents
2. what is directly supported by structured customer data
3. what remains unknown

## 6. Final reminder
This policy is only a synthetic demo aid. It must not be used for real credit, lending, or risk decisions.
